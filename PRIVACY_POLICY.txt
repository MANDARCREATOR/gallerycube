========================================================================
   GALLERY CUBE — PRIVACY POLICY
   Publisher: rmscube
   Effective Date: 2026-11-11

1. OVERVIEW & COMMITMENT
   rmscube ("we", "us", or "our") develops Gallery Cube ("the Application")
   with a strict Privacy-First, Zero-Data-Collection architectural design.
   This policy explains our data protection practices under:
   - The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)
   - The Information Technology Act, 2000 and SPDI Rules (India)
   - The General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR)
   - The California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA (United States)
   - International Personal Data Protection frameworks

2. ZERO DATA COLLECTION
   - The Application is a 100% offline desktop application.
   - We do NOT collect, store, transmit, process, or sell any personal data.
   - We do NOT collect telemetry, analytics, device identifiers, or crash logs.
   - We do NOT track what photos or videos you open, view, edit, or save.

3. LOCAL PROCESSING OF MEDIA & EXIF DATA
   - All image and video processing, rendering, editing, and conversion takes
     place entirely on your local computer's processor and memory.
   - Any camera parameters, EXIF tags, or GPS coordinates embedded in your
     photos remain strictly within your local environment.
   - Gallery Cube never uploads your media files to any cloud server or remote
     infrastructure.

4. NETWORK ACCESS
   - The Application does not contain hidden background network connections
     or phone-home mechanisms.
   - The only optional network action occurs if you explicitly click
     "Open in Google Maps" inside the Camera Info inspector to view GPS
     coordinates in your default web browser.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
   - Because the Application collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users
     of all ages, complying fully with COPPA (US) and relevant global standards.

6. CONTACT
   - Publisher: rmscube
   - Inquiries regarding privacy compliance: legal@rmscube.com
